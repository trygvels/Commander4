# M2.4 implementation plan: hierarchical index field in the Commander4 per-pixel path

Synthesis of two drafts (A: minimal, B: clean architecture), checked against the code on branch
`nextgen` at HEAD d63e013. Paths are relative to /Users/trygvels/code/Commander4. Stage 1 is the
first milestone (M2.4a, reaches ladder L3 with the smallest change). Stage 2 (M2.4b) is the
follow-on that leaves the clean long-term architecture. Nothing in either stage changes the result
of a parameter file that does not set the new keys (CLAUDE.md principle 7, D003 unchanged).

## 1. Target distribution (stated once, both stages sample exactly this)

Per polarization view (I, or QU with Q and U as two streams sharing one index field), data pixel p
at the common nside_d after `common_res_fwhm` smoothing, bands b of the one per-pixel amplitude
group, components c in that group's `comps` (fixed components subtracted through
`_build_conditional_residual`, processing.py:481 to 504):

    d_p = M_p a_p + n_p,   n_p ~ N(0, N_p) diagonal,   M_p[b, c] = sed_c(nu_b; beta_c[r_c(p)])

- Index field per sampled component c: beta_c = mu_beta 1 + sigma S(rho) z, z ~ N(0, I_K),
  K = Nb - 1 (constant mode dropped), graph-Laplacian basis, Matern alpha = 2, eigenvalues scaled
  by 1/(3 h^2), c(rho) normalization (D009). r_c(p) = nest parent of p at nside_beta_c, -1 under
  the group mask. Hyperpriors: mu_beta ~ N(mu0, sd0^2) with (mu0, sd0) from
  `spectral_index_prior`, sigma ~ half-N(0, 0.3) sampled as log sigma with Jacobian, rho
  log-uniform on [h, pi] through u = logit with Jacobian. nside_beta = 0: one index,
  beta ~ N(mu0, sd0^2), no field block (E017 scalar target, today's prior).
- Amplitude prior per component, `amp_prior.type`: `none` (flat, today), `gaussian`
  (a_p ~ N(m, t^2) fixed; cmb default m = 0, t = 70 uK_RJ), `hier` (a_p ~ N(mu_c g_p,c,
  (s_c g_p,c)^2), g a fixed strictly positive template at the component nu_ref in uK_RJ at
  nside_d; mu_c ~ N(1, 0.5^2), log s_c ~ N(log 0.3, 1^2); log s_c < -8 has zero density).
- Collapsed likelihood, A'_p = A_p + T_p^-1, b'_p = b_p + T_p^-1 m_p (flat rows contribute 0):

      log L(beta, mu, s) = sum_p [1/2 b'^T A'^-1 b' - 1/2 log det A'] - 1/2 sum_p m^T T^-1 m
                           - 1/2 sum_p log det T + const

  const = -1/2 d^T N^-1 d - (B/2) log 2 pi - 1/2 log det N (stats-reviewer CONFIRMED, E016).
  The trailing terms are constant only for `none` and `gaussian`; they are kept for `hier`.
  Masked pixels (region -1) contribute 0 and are excluded.
- Joint target over the packed vector x = (field block of each sampled c, mu_beta_c, log sigma_c,
  u_c, mu_c, log s_c of each hier c), p(x | d) prop. to L N(z; 0, I) p(hypers). Sampled by NUTS.
  Non-centred by default (passed SBC at K 192, E012); centred as an option, same target up to
  the Jacobian -K log sigma - 1/2 log det K.
- Amplitude Gibbs step: a_p ~ N(A'^-1 b', A'^-1) exactly, replacing today's flat `solve_perpix`
  draw. With `amp_prior: none` everywhere and `likelihood: ridge` the chain is today's chain.

This section goes to the stats-reviewer before commit 5 of stage 1 (CLAUDE.md principle 6).

## 2. Corrections to the drafts (checked against the code)

- Amplitude draw cannot be bypassed (draft A section 3.5). `process_compsep` runs every
  amplitude group before the MCMC groups each iteration (processing.py:768 to 775) and
  `resolve_amplitudes` reruns `_run_amplitude_group` (778 to 782), which calls
  `solve_compsep_perpix` with one flat-prior M (perpix_solver.py:90 to 99). The per-region,
  prior-aware draw must live inside `solve_compsep_perpix`, as the roadmap says ("per-region
  mixing in `solve_perpix`") and as draft B step 6 has it.
- Polarization (draft B risk 3 and draft A risk 3). A QU rank's `PerPixelMarginalLikelihood`
  holds Q and U as two streams on the same solver root (perpix_solver.py:173 to 182), and the I
  and QU totals are already allreduced over `compsep_comm` (perpix_solver.py:202, mcmc.py:165).
  One field per view, shared by the Q and U streams; no I-only restriction is needed.
- `beta` stays a plain attribute (against draft B). `hasattr(comp, "beta")` gates discovery
  (spectral_index.py:114) and restart (component.py:220); `write_chain` reads it through
  `sed_param_names` (component.py:196 to 199); the view join compares it (164 to 167). With a
  field, `beta` holds the field mean as the scalar fallback (draft A).
- `sed/beta` stays scalar or [I, QU] (against draft B): `read_chain` treats any 1-D array as the
  [I, QU] pair (component.py:221 to 224) and aux/validate_mcmc_likelihood.py:262 reads `[0]`.
  The field goes in new datasets (section 6).
- `CompSepState` is `frozen=True` (processing.py:170), built once (576). Persistent NUTS state
  goes in a new mutable `IndexSamplerState` held next to it in cli.py (constructed at 110 to
  130), or in a dict-valued field mutated in place; it is not an attribute to assign.
- Map-valued SED for the `spatially_varying_MM` branch must be at band.nside and band.lmax
  (diffuse_components.py:399 to 406), not at the component nside.
- The region kernel skips pixels with fewer than ncomp bands of positive inverse variance
  (compsep_pymod.cc:305 to 309); pseudo-bands count as observed, so with a proper prior on every
  component nothing is skipped; with a flat cmb and fewer than one observed band a pixel is still
  skipped (document; report nskipped in the chain).
- No stored-hash regression of a stochastic MPI run (draft A). The guard for unchanged behaviour
  is the existing tests/test_mcmc_likelihood.py toy and tests/test_compsep_chain_content.py.

## 3. Stage 1 (M2.4a): smallest change that reaches L3

Prior by augmentation (draft A), region kernel reused as is, amplitude draw fixed in place.

1. C++ (src/lib_cpp/compsep_pymod.cc, about 90 lines, old bindings byte identical):
   a. `solve_perpix_regions(map_sky, map_inv_var, region, M_reg, rand, nthreads)`: the body of
      `Py_solve_perpix` (93 to 135) with M_reg[region[p]] per pixel, region < 0 gives zeros;
      share the per-pixel body through a template on "M for pixel p". Stub in
      src/commander4/_cmdr4_backend/compsep.pyi.
   b. `marginal_loglike_regions`: optional `want_extras` returning x (ncomp, npix) and
      diag(A^-1) (ncomp, npix), zeros where skipped. Return values unchanged when false.
   The Gaussian and hier priors enter as C pseudo-bands appended to each stream: map = m_p,c,
   inv_var = 1/T_p,c (0 for flat components), M_reg row = unit vector e_c, dM_reg row = 0. The
   kernel's (A, b) are then exactly (A', b'), the gradient is the gauss envelope gradient, and
   `solve_perpix_regions` on the augmented stream draws from N(A'^-1 b', A'^-1) with no extra
   random vector (eta on a pseudo-band is T^-1/2 eta_2). One kernel to validate, not two.
2. src/commander4/math_utils/gmrf_field.py (new, about 260 lines): port of
   nextgen/prototypes/indexfield/field.py (`GraphLaplacianBasis`, `MaternField`, `RhoTransform`)
   plus the hyperprior terms of posterior.py `HyperPrior` and `_hyper_terms`, same names, NumPy
   and healpy only, docstrings with the math, 100-character lines. nside_beta capped at 16
   (dense eigh of 3072 x 3072); no disk cache in stage 1.
3. src/commander4/compsep/index_field.py (new, about 350 lines):
   - `AmpPrior` dataclass (type, m or g at nside_d, mu, log_s, hyperprior parameters) and
     `augment_stream(freqs, maps, inv_var, comps) -> (maps_aug, inv_var_aug, pseudo_rows)`.
   - `RegionIndexLikelihood`: mirrors `PerPixelMarginalLikelihood` (gather once per group run
     to the solver root, perpix_solver.py:165 to 182; region map at nside_d; augmented streams).
     `logp_and_grad(x)`: unpack per view, beta per index pixel, `M_reg`/`dM_reg` from
     `comp.get_sed(freqs, beta=...)` and `comp.get_dsed_dbeta(...)`, kernel with extras, trailing
     terms and (mu, log s) gradients as model.py `hier_loglike` 203 to 210, chain rule as
     posterior.py `NonCentredPosterior`/`CentredPosterior`, hyperpriors and Jacobians, then
     allreduce (logp, grad) over `compsep_comm` with the gradient zero outside this rank's own
     block. Non-PD pixel or non-finite value gives (-inf, 0) (nuts.py contract, lines 15 to 16).
   - `IndexFieldSamplingGroup`: root runs `NUTSSampler.transition` `numstep` times; other ranks
     serve (bcast x, evaluate, until a stop token sent from a `finally` block). Returns stats:
     step_size, accept_stat, n_leapfrog, tree_depth, divergent, and the packed vector.
   - `pack/unpack` helpers and `IndexSamplerState` (NUTSSampler and current x per group, kept
     across Gibbs iterations so warm-up spans the first n_warmup iterations).
4. src/commander4/sky/diffuse_components.py (about 80 lines): `get_sed(nu, beta=None)` for
   ThermalDust and Synchrotron broadcasts an index array to (nreg, nband);
   `get_dsed_dbeta(nu, beta)` = sed * ln(nu/nu_ref) for both (dust: T fixed). New attributes
   `beta_map: ndarray | None` (Nb,), `beta_nside: int`, `amp_prior: AmpPrior | None`; `beta` kept

   `eval_comp_from_band` take the existing `spatially_varying_MM` branch (set automatically) with
   `get_sed_map(nu, nside)` = SED of `ud_grade(beta_map, band.nside)`, so the conditional
   residual, output maps and `conditional` mode see the field.
5. src/commander4/compsep/perpix_solver.py (about 60 lines): `solve_compsep_perpix` builds the
   region map and `M_reg` per view from `beta_map` (nreg = 1 when no component has a field),
   augments the stream with the priors, calls `solve_perpix_regions`; alms as today (104 to 115,
   F7 caveat unchanged). With no field and no prior the call reduces to `solve_perpix` (tested

6. src/commander4/compsep/processing.py (about 120 lines): `likelihood: hier` in

7. Chain output and restart (about 60 lines): `comps/<shortname>/sed/beta` = mean (unchanged

## 7. Risks

- Pseudo-band skip semantics: a proper prior on every component means no skipped pixels; a flat
  cmb with hier sync/dust is allowed but flagged "not validated" and its nskipped is logged.
- cmb degeneracy at low S/N (E012 noise 3): keep N(0, 70^2) for L3; report cmb amplitude bias
  per S/N bin in E018.
- Mass adaptation across blocks of different scale: start `inv_mass` from the prior scales
  (z 1, log sigma 0.3^2, u 1, mu_c 0.5^2, log s_c 1); E-BFMI recorded.
- Non-stationary conditional in the full Gibbs loop: stage 1 is validated in the maps-only path
  with one per-pixel group where nothing else moves; the TOD loop is M2.5.
- Cost: all on the solver root as today; one gradient at nside 256 with 20 bands is about 1.5 s
  single thread, so M2.4a targets nside 16 to 64; the distributed kernel is stage 2.
- Pivot: g must be given at nu_ref (E016); `amp_prior/nu_ref` is written to the chain and checked.
- Root exception with serving ranks: stop token in `finally`, tested with a raising likelihood.
- F7 and F8 remain; L3 avoids them by construction, L4 will not.

## Judge's verdict on the two drafts

Plan A (minimal): (1) says the hier group "skips the usual amplitude groups since the draw replaced them", but process_compsep runs every amplitude group before the MCMC groups each iteration (processing.py:768 to 775) and `resolve_amplitudes` reruns `_run_amplitude_group` (778 to 782), which calls `solve_compsep_perpix` with one flat-prior M (perpix_solver.py:90 to 99); the amplitude draw must be made region- and prior-aware inside `solve_compsep_perpix`, not bypassed. (2) "one field per stream (I, QU)" is wrong wording: a QU rank holds Q and U as two streams on the same solver root (perpix_solver.py:173 to 182); the field is per view and shared by both streams. (3) The map-valued `get_sed` for the `spatially_varying_MM` branch must be at band.nside, not "the component nside" (diffuse_components.py:399 to 406). (4) `CompSepState` is `frozen=True` (processing.py:170), so a persistent NUTS sampler cannot simply be assigned to it. (5) A "stored hash of the current ridge run" is not a regression test for a stochastic 15-minute MPI run; use the toy and chain-content tests.

Plan B (clean): (1) Making `beta` a property that raises on scalar read breaks `hasattr(comp, "beta")` at spectral_index.py:114 and component.py:220 (an AttributeError silently drops the component from discovery) and crashes `write_chain` (component.py:196 to 199) and the view join (164 to 167). (2) Writing `sed/beta` as the region vector breaks `read_chain`, which treats any 1-D array as an [I, QU] pair (component.py:221 to 224), and aux/validate_mcmc_likelihood.py:262. (3) "First version supports I only and refuses QU" rests on a false premise: Q and U already share one root and the I and QU totals are already allreduced over compsep_comm (perpix_solver.py:202, mcmc.py:165). (4) A +250 line prior-aware C++ kernel and `solve_perpix_regions` with `rand_prior` duplicate what pseudo-band augmentation of the existing `marginal_loglike_regions` gives exactly (A' = A + T^-1, b' = b + T^-1 m, zero dM rows), at the cost of a second kernel to validate. (5) Redundant config surface: `sampler: nuts` plus `likelihood: hier`, and `index_field.prior.mu` as an alias of `spectral_index_prior`, give two ways to say one thing; the same frozen `CompSepState` problem as A.
