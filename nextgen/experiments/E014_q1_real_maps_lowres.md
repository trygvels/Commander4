# E014: Does the legacy acceptance change spectral-index posteriors on real low-resolution maps? (Q1)

- **Date started / finished:** 2026-10-25 /
- **Branch and commit:** nextgen, 778ae58 plus the scripts added in this record
- **Open question addressed:** Q1 (M1.3, M1.4)
- **Validation level:** L5-type real-data comparison between likelihood modes; the reference is the
  `ridge` chain (exact for its target, F2), with `conditional` as a second exact chain for a
  different target and `resolve_chisq` as the legacy move.

## Question
On public maps (Cosmoglobe DR1 WMAP + LFI, Planck PR4 HFI, Haslam 408 MHz) at nside 64 and 120
arcmin, how far do the full-sky synchrotron and dust index posteriors from Commander4's legacy
`resolve_chisq` move sit from the exact `ridge` and `conditional` posteriors, in units of the ridge
posterior sd, and how do widths compare? Decides the urgency and framing of the upstream fix (Q13).

## Target and priors
Commander4 maps-only per-pixel model, intensity only in the first pass: per pixel p at nside 64,
d_{b,p} = sum_c M_{b,c}(beta_s, beta_d) a_{c,p} + n_{b,p}, white noise with the smoothed inverse
variance Commander4 propagates (F8 caveat), components CMB, synchrotron (power law, nu_ref 30 GHz),
thermal dust (MBB, T = 20 K fixed, nu_ref 545 GHz), free-free (T_e = 7000 K fixed), spinning dust
(SpDust2 CNM template, pysm2 `emissivity.txt`, peak fixed at 25.3 GHz). Full-sky scalar beta_s with
flat prior on [-4, -2]; beta_d with N(1.56, 0.1^2) prior on [1.0, 2.5]. Per-pixel amplitudes with
flat priors.
- `ridge`: MH on the profile likelihood (amplitudes at their per-pixel ML), target
  p_ridge(beta | d) proportional to exp(-chi2_min(beta)/2) times the priors.
- `conditional`: MH on chi2 at the current amplitudes, then a Gibbs amplitude draw; target is the
  flat-amplitude-prior joint posterior, whose beta marginal carries the volume term.
- `marginal`: MH on the flat-prior marginal (ridge minus 1/2 sum_p ln det A_p).
- `resolve_chisq`: legacy move (propose, redraw amplitudes, accept on chi2 at the draws); no valid
  stationary target (F1).

## Setup
- Data (first pass; the second pass replaces PR4 HFI by NPIPE A 353/857 from UiO plus PR3 100 GHz, see Result): see `nextgen/DATA.md` local copies. DR1 maps are dipole- and monopole-corrected copies
  made by `nextgen/scripts/prep_q1_inputs.py` (Planck 2018 solar dipole subtracted; a high-latitude
  monopole per band subtracted as a stand-in for Commander3's per-band monopole sampling, which
  Commander4 lacks). WMAP DR1 maps are in mK, LFI in uK (the DR1 headers say so; the upstream
  Commander4 parameter files have Ka to W in mK too). PR4 HFI maps are converted by
  `nextgen/scripts/prep_pr4_maps.py` (plain-average degrade to nside 512 with exact white-noise
  variance propagation). Haslam map and rms are the BeyondPlanck inputs (`haslam_n0512_60arcmin_uK`,
  rms with 1 percent plus 0.8 K).
- Resolution: `eval_nside: 64` (Commander4 degrades on read, inverse variances summed), common beam
  120 arcmin (pixel size 55 arcmin), lmax 191 (F7 applies to the per-pixel amplitude storage).
- Parameter files: generated by `nextgen/scripts/make_q1_params.py` into `nextgen/params/q1/`, one
  per mode; intensity only (`--pol I`); 2000 Gibbs iterations, 10 MH steps per iteration, same
  seed and start values for all modes. 14 MPI ranks without HFI (one per band), 20 with HFI.
- Machine: Mac mini, 10 cores.

## Reference
Modes `ridge`, `conditional` and `marginal` are exact for their targets (F2, E005, E006). The
comparison statistics are posterior means and sds of beta_s and beta_d after burn-in, ESS by
autocorrelation time, and the shift of `resolve_chisq` from `ridge` in ridge sds. An independent
grid evaluation of the ridge posterior on the same per-pixel data (as in E006) is used to confirm
the ridge chain, computed from the chain's smoothed band maps.

## Result
(pending)

Smoke tests (3 iterations, ridge, no HFI): with the raw DR1 maps the reduced chi2 was 1.2e6 (the
solar dipole of 3.35 mK was still in the maps and the WMAP units were wrong); after dipole and
monopole removal 447; with spinning dust enabled 155 (Planck 30 GHz 293, WMAP Ka 758, Haslam 5).

**First pass without HFI (stopped after 875 iterations, 2026-10-25): not usable.** The ridge chain
ran beta_d to its hard bound (1.5116 at iteration 1, 1.009 at 10, 1.0000 from 50 on) and beta_s to
-2.806, then accepted nothing (acceptance 0.0 for 800 iterations; reduced chi2 153.8). Cause: with
no band above 94 GHz the dust index is unconstrained and the per-pixel white-noise likelihood at
nside 64 (noise ~1 uK per pixel against model residuals of tens of uK) turns small model errors
into chi2 differences of thousands, which overwhelm the N(1.56, 0.1) prior and pin the index to a
bound. The comparison between likelihood modes is meaningless in that state, so the other three
modes were not run. Fix for the second pass: HFI bands (PR3 from the Planck Legacy Archive, PR4 at
NERSC transfers at 30 kB/s), a Galactic chi-square mask for the index likelihood (the dx12 common
mask at nside 64, 0.9 threshold, and a |b| > 20 deg fallback are in `~/data/q1_inputs/`), and a
model-error floor on the rms (`add_signal_fraction_to_rms`) if the chains still sit on bounds.

Masked test (150 iterations, ridge, no HFI, dx12 common mask at nside 64 for the index chi2,
1 percent rms floor): beta_d still at the bound (1.000), beta_s -3.17 (more sensible than -2.81),
acceptance 0.0 to 0.1, reduced chi2 153.6 (the 1 percent floor is about 1 uK at 30 GHz and
changes nothing). Two lessons: HFI is required for beta_d, and the proposal widths (0.01 / 0.02)
are far too wide for a full-sky index constrained by 3072 pixels at about 1 uK noise, where the
ridge posterior sd is of order 1e-3; the second pass uses 0.002 / 0.004 and `numstep` 20.


**Second pass, input preparation (2026-10-25 evening).** The Planck Legacy Archive drops PR3
transfers above about 60 MB for 143 and 217 GHz (100 GHz verified), so the HFI set is the NPIPE
A-split 353 and 857 GHz maps hosted at UiO as BeyondPlanck inputs (`npipe6v20A_*`, 3.2 GB) plus
PR3 100 GHz. Four things had to be fixed before the fit made sense (`prep_pr4_maps.py --release
npipe_uio`), each found from a chain that sat at a bound or at reduced chi-square above 1e4:

1. *Units.* The UiO files say K_CMB in the header but hold uK_CMB (file name, pixel rms 30 uK at
   353 GHz, nside 512). Taken as uK_CMB.
2. *Dipole.* NPIPE frequency maps keep the solar dipole: a fit at |b| > 30 deg to the 353 GHz map
   returns 3311 uK toward (l, b) = (262.6, 47.5). The Planck 2018 dipole is subtracted.
3. *857 GHz unit convention.* BeyondPlanck converted the 857 GHz MJy/sr map to K_CMB with the
   band-integrated HFI factor (2.27 MJy/sr per K_CMB); Commander4 converts a K_CMB band back to
   brightness with the monochromatic dB/dT at 857 GHz (1.44 MJy/sr per K_CMB), which makes the band
   1.58x too faint and drives beta_d to its lower bound (1.0007, acceptance 0). Verified by
   regressing the 353 and 857 maps on the Cosmoglobe DR1 dust template at |b| > 30 deg: the slopes
   agree to 4 percent only after the 1.58 correction. The 857 GHz map is therefore written in MJy/sr
   (BP's conversion undone), which Commander4 converts monochromatically as for the PR3 files. At
   353 GHz the same effect is 2.5 percent and is left as a colour-correction error of the delta
   bandpass (noted; Commander3 integrates the bandpass).
4. *Zero levels.* Commander4 has no monopole sampling. 353 and 857 GHz: offset of a least-squares fit
   of (map minus DR1 CMB, dipole removed) against the DR1 dust template at |b| > 30 deg after
   2 deg smoothing, the HI-correlation idea with the DR1 dust model as tracer (447.9 uK_CMB at
   353 GHz; -0.56e6 uK_CMB, i.e. +1.28 MJy/sr, at 857 GHz). 100 GHz: mean over |b| > 30 deg
   (25.7 uK), as for the DR1 WMAP and LFI maps.

Anomaly, resolved (F13): under the header units (uK_RJ, nu_ref 545 GHz) the DR1 dust amplitude
looked 7 to 12x too low (857 GHz in uK_RJ regressed on `I_MEAN` at |b| > 30 deg: slope 12.2,
correlation 0.994). The amplitude is referenced at 70 GHz: `param_CG_v09.txt` line 1774 overrides
`COMP_NU_REF_T03 = 70 857` and Commander3 reads the first token. Predicted slopes for a 70 GHz
reference with the DR1 per-pixel beta and T are 11.3 at 857 GHz and 7.3 at 353 GHz against 12.2 and
7.2 measured (an independent agent reproduced the regressions; polarization gives 8.3 vs 7.3). The
zero-level fits here use the template with a free scale and are unaffected; `prep_pr4_maps.py` now
uses 70 GHz (`DR1_DUST_NU_REF`). Details in DATA.md.

Model-error floor: `add_signal_fraction_to_rms: 0.05` on the three HFI bands (Commander3 adds
regularization noise to HFI for the same reason). Dust T stays fixed at 20 K.

(This reconstruction uses a plain-average degrade and Gaussian smoothing, not Commander4's

launched 18:31 with these inputs: ridge, resolve_chisq, conditional, marginal, 2000 iterations.

**Pass 2, ridge, 2000 iterations (18:31 to 20:08, 17 ranks):** beta_d 1.36539 +- 0.00005, beta_s
-3.3425 +- 0.0004 (burn 400, one Commander4 chain written). The chain reached the mode by iteration
50 and then accepted 0.4 percent of proposals (117 distinct values in 1600 iterations, ESS 44 and
97): the proposals 0.004 / 0.002 are 80x and 6x the posterior sds, which at nside 64 full-sky are
of order 1e-4. The mean is well determined (standard error about 1e-5) but the width is known to
about 15 percent only. A tuned round (proposals 1.2e-4 / 9e-4, about 2.4 sd, 1000 iterations, tag
`npipetuned`) is queued behind the untuned driver for all four modes; the untuned chains of the
other three modes are kept because the comparison in E005 used one proposal width for all modes.
Reduced chi-square 14994 (Galactic plane, outside the index mask).

**Exact grid targets on the same data (`nextgen/scripts/q1_grid_check.py`, 17 bands, 38165 kept
pixels, Commander4's own reader, smoothing, mask and SEDs, no MH code; coarse 41 x 41 grid over
the prior box then shrinking 41 x 41 grids to a step of 0.3 sd):**

| target | beta_s | beta_d |
|---|---|---|
| ridge (profile, flat amplitude priors) | -3.34244 +- 0.00036 | 1.36539 +- 0.000043 |
| flat-prior marginal (ridge minus 1/2 sum ln det A_p) | -3.32314 +- 0.00033 | 1.36562 +- 0.000043 |

The ridge chain agrees with its exact target: shift -0.03 +- 0.10 sd in beta_s and -0.17 +- 0.18
sd in beta_d, width ratios 1.01 and 1.18 (the dust ESS is 44, so the width is uncertain at the 15
percent level). F2 therefore holds on real data at nside 64. The volume term of the flat amplitude
prior moves the exact beta_s target by +0.019, which is 54 ridge sd, and beta_d by +0.0002 (5 sd):
on these maps the prior choice (Q2, F3) matters far more than the sampler width. The `conditional`
and `marginal` chains should land on the marginal row; `resolve_chisq` has no target.

**Pass 2, resolve_chisq (legacy move), 2000 iterations (20:08 to 05:55, 9.8 h, 17 ranks):**
beta_d 1.3655 +- 0.0007, beta_s -3.343 +- 0.006, acceptance 0.073 (burn 400; ESS 1184 and 74).
Against the exact ridge target on the same data:

| | beta_s | beta_d |
|---|---|---|
| exact ridge | -3.34244 +- 0.00036 | 1.36539 +- 0.000043 |
| ridge chain | shift -0.03 +- 0.10 sd, width ratio 1.01 | shift -0.17 +- 0.18 sd, width ratio 1.18 |
| resolve_chisq chain | shift -2.7 +- 2.0 sd, width ratio 17.1 | shift +3.0 +- 0.5 sd, width ratio 16.5 |

The legacy move's posterior is 16 to 17x too wide for both indices on these real maps (38165
masked pixels at nside 64, five components, 17 bands), and its mean is shifted by 3.0 +- 0.5 ridge
sd in beta_d (6 sigma); the beta_s shift of -2.7 sd is not significant at ESS 74. The width
inflation is far above the 3.2x to 4.4x of the 150 to 600 pixel unit tests (E005) and follows the
k^(1/4) scaling of F1 taken at face value (k about 1.9e5 amplitude degrees of freedom: (1.9e5 /
600)^(1/4) x 4.4 = 18.5). Both chains used the same proposal widths (0.004 / 0.002), which are
2.4 sd for the legacy chain and 80 / 6 sd for the ridge chain; the legacy posterior is wide enough
to be sampled well by them, which is why its acceptance (0.073) is larger than ridge's (0.004). The
reduced chi-square of the two chains is the same (14989 vs 14994): the sky fit does not tell the
two apart, only the index posterior does.

Answer to Q1 at this resolution: on real data, Commander4's legacy index move overstates the index
uncertainty by a factor of 16 to 17 and shifts the dust index by several (correct) sd. At nside 512
to 1024 (k 100 to 400x larger) the F1 scaling predicts factors above 50. The `conditional` and
`marginal` chains (running) measure the prior effect separately.

`resolve_chisq` is the known-wrong method; E005/E006 predict it is wider and shifted.
