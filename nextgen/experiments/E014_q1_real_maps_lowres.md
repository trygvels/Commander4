# E014: Does the legacy acceptance change spectral-index posteriors on real low-resolution maps? (Q1)

- **Date started / finished:** 2026-10-25 /
- **Branch and commit:** nextgen, 778ae58 plus the scripts added in this record
- **Open question addressed:** Q1 (M1.3, M1.4)
- **Validation level:** L5-type real-data comparison between likelihood modes; the reference is the
  `ridge` chain (exact for its target, F2), with `conditional` as a second exact chain for a
  different target and `resolve_chisq` as the legacy move.

## Question
On public maps (Cosmoglobe DR1 WMAP + LFI, Planck PR4 HFI, Haslam 408 MHz) at nside 64 and 120
arcmin, how far do the full-sky synchrotron and dust index posteriors from Commander4's legacy
`resolve_chisq` move sit from the exact `ridge` and `conditional` posteriors, in units of the ridge
posterior sd, and how do widths compare? Decides the urgency and framing of the upstream fix (Q13).

## Target and priors
Commander4 maps-only per-pixel model, intensity only in the first pass: per pixel p at nside 64,
d_{b,p} = sum_c M_{b,c}(beta_s, beta_d) a_{c,p} + n_{b,p}, white noise with the smoothed inverse
variance Commander4 propagates (F8 caveat), components CMB, synchrotron (power law, nu_ref 30 GHz),
thermal dust (MBB, T = 20 K fixed, nu_ref 545 GHz), free-free (T_e = 7000 K fixed), spinning dust
(SpDust2 CNM template, pysm2 `emissivity.txt`, peak fixed at 25.3 GHz). Full-sky scalar beta_s with
flat prior on [-4, -2]; beta_d with N(1.56, 0.1^2) prior on [1.0, 2.5]. Per-pixel amplitudes with
flat priors.
- `ridge`: MH on the profile likelihood (amplitudes at their per-pixel ML), target
  p_ridge(beta | d) proportional to exp(-chi2_min(beta)/2) times the priors.
- `conditional`: MH on chi2 at the current amplitudes, then a Gibbs amplitude draw; target is the
  flat-amplitude-prior joint posterior, whose beta marginal carries the volume term.
- `marginal`: MH on the flat-prior marginal (ridge minus 1/2 sum_p ln det A_p).
- `resolve_chisq`: legacy move (propose, redraw amplitudes, accept on chi2 at the draws); no valid
  stationary target (F1).

## Setup
- Data: see `nextgen/DATA.md` local copies. DR1 maps are dipole- and monopole-corrected copies
  made by `nextgen/scripts/prep_q1_inputs.py` (Planck 2018 solar dipole subtracted; a high-latitude
  monopole per band subtracted as a stand-in for Commander3's per-band monopole sampling, which
  Commander4 lacks). WMAP DR1 maps are in mK, LFI in uK (the DR1 headers say so; the upstream
  Commander4 parameter files have Ka to W in mK too). PR4 HFI maps are converted by
  `nextgen/scripts/prep_pr4_maps.py` (plain-average degrade to nside 512 with exact white-noise
  variance propagation). Haslam map and rms are the BeyondPlanck inputs (`haslam_n0512_60arcmin_uK`,
  rms with 1 percent plus 0.8 K).
- Resolution: `eval_nside: 64` (Commander4 degrades on read, inverse variances summed), common beam
  120 arcmin (pixel size 55 arcmin), lmax 191 (F7 applies to the per-pixel amplitude storage).
- Parameter files: generated by `nextgen/scripts/make_q1_params.py` into `nextgen/params/q1/`, one
  per mode; intensity only (`--pol I`); 2000 Gibbs iterations, 10 MH steps per iteration, same
  seed and start values for all modes. 14 MPI ranks without HFI (one per band), 20 with HFI.
- Machine: Mac mini, 10 cores.

## Reference
Modes `ridge`, `conditional` and `marginal` are exact for their targets (F2, E005, E006). The
comparison statistics are posterior means and sds of beta_s and beta_d after burn-in, ESS by
autocorrelation time, and the shift of `resolve_chisq` from `ridge` in ridge sds. An independent
grid evaluation of the ridge posterior on the same per-pixel data (as in E006) is used to confirm
the ridge chain, computed from the chain's smoothed band maps.

## Result
(pending)

Smoke tests (3 iterations, ridge, no HFI): with the raw DR1 maps the reduced chi2 was 1.2e6 (the
solar dipole of 3.35 mK was still in the maps and the WMAP units were wrong); after dipole and
monopole removal 447; with spinning dust enabled 155 (Planck 30 GHz 293, WMAP Ka 758, Haslam 5).

**First pass without HFI (stopped after 875 iterations, 2026-10-25): not usable.** The ridge chain
ran beta_d to its hard bound (1.5116 at iteration 1, 1.009 at 10, 1.0000 from 50 on) and beta_s to
-2.806, then accepted nothing (acceptance 0.0 for 800 iterations; reduced chi2 153.8). Cause: with
no band above 94 GHz the dust index is unconstrained and the per-pixel white-noise likelihood at
nside 64 (noise ~1 uK per pixel against model residuals of tens of uK) turns small model errors
into chi2 differences of thousands, which overwhelm the N(1.56, 0.1) prior and pin the index to a
bound. The comparison between likelihood modes is meaningless in that state, so the other three
modes were not run. Fix for the second pass: HFI bands (PR3 from the Planck Legacy Archive, PR4 at
NERSC transfers at 30 kB/s), a Galactic chi-square mask for the index likelihood (the dx12 common
mask at nside 64, 0.9 threshold, and a |b| > 20 deg fallback are in `~/data/q1_inputs/`), and a
model-error floor on the rms (`add_signal_fraction_to_rms`) if the chains still sit on bounds.
